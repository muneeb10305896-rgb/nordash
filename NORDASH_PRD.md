## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

**NORDASH** is a Nordic-inspired, globally-focused digital agency that bridges Scandinavian minimalism with Pakistani/South Asian creative energy. We deliver high-impact digital services: video editing, thumbnail design, social media marketing, software development, brand strategy, and UI/UX design.

Our value proposition: **"Where Nordic Design Meets Truck Art"** — combining clean, professional Scandinavian aesthetics with bold, colorful South Asian visual storytelling.

**Target Markets:**
- Mid-market tech companies (Series A-B startups)
- E-commerce brands needing content velocity
- Personal brands & content creators scaling
- Digital-first B2B SaaS companies
- Creative agencies needing white-label partnerships

**Revenue Model:**
- Service-based (hourly/retainer) — primary
- Project-based (fixed scope) — secondary
- Potential: productized services (templates, courses)
